About the foundation

Built for timely help, trust, and measurable community impact.

The foundation works from Dudhdewadi, Taluka Karad, District Satara, Maharashtra, with a clear purpose: to connect people with practical support during difficult moments and to encourage organized social service.

The mobile app and website together create a transparent channel for membership, service requests, activity updates, volunteer participation, and donation readiness.
